# Capstone 12: AI Research Report on the Semiconductor Industry

**Topic:** EV chips (SiC/GaN power semiconductors), the global supply chain and India's position
**Author:** Abinaya R
**Course:** AI Skills Course (Topics 1-9), Electronics and Communication Engineering

## Research questions
1. Which chips are used in an EV, and what do SiC and GaN offer over silicon in efficiency and cost?
2. Who controls the EV chip supply chain, and what is India's position (ISM, Tata Electronics, CG Power)?
3. What are the main trends and risks (shortage, cost, import dependency)?

## Method
The same three questions were put to four AI tools (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude). Every answer was split into single claims and checked against government, company, research and news pages. Numbers were recomputed by hand, and every cited source was re-opened before submission. AI answers were never used as sources.

## Key findings
- SiC beats silicon in EV inverters, but the gain depends on the test (over 1% in simulation; up to 3% in a vendor claim).
- Four companies held 82% of SiC substrate revenue in 2024; the top five held 91.9% of SiC power devices in 2023.
- India imports 90-95% of its semiconductor demand; 12 projects are approved, including the first commercial SiC fab (Bhubaneswar).
- Approved is not producing: yields, automotive qualification and customers come next.

## Responsible AI
Only public information was used. Sources are paraphrased and cited. 24 AI errors and unsupported claims are documented in the Submission Pack.
